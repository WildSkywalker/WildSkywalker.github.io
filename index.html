About me

I am a Research Data Scientist at Google. I work on LLM evaluation and AI metrics used across Google, including by Google DeepMind. I lead the Work Done by AI team, which develops metrics that track AI participation in workflows across the company.

Since joining my current team in February 2026, I have worked on coding-agent trajectory scoring as part of the evaluation effort behind Gemini 4 Argon.

I earned my Ph.D. in mathematics at Northwestern University (before the “super math AI” era), where I was advised by Antonio Auffinger. I’m a probabilist, interested in just about anything involving probability and its applications, from computer science to statistical physics. Outside research, I play the violin, which I have studied since the age of four.
